An independent software studio.

Brow Nouveau, LLC builds careful, privacy-respecting software. Small teams, considered tools, and a long view on what's worth shipping.

What we do

We design and operate a small portfolio of consumer and productivity applications. Our work tends toward the personal and the practical — software that fits cleanly into the rhythm of someone's day rather than competing for it.

We work in the open where we can, hold our own infrastructure where it matters, and prefer durable engineering over fashionable engineering.

How we work

  • Privacy by default. We collect what we need to deliver the product and nothing else. Encryption at rest where appropriate; no analytics or third-party tracking in our consumer products.
  • Small, owned, durable. We build to last rather than to scale prematurely. Fewer dependencies, fewer abstractions, code we can still read in five years.
  • Plain language. Our documentation, terms, and policies are written to be read.
